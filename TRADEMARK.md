# Qyic Trademark & Brand Guidelines

**Last Updated:** October 2026

## 1. Overview: Open Source Code vs. Protected Brand

The software code in this repository is released under the **Apache License 2.0**. You are free to inspect, fork, modify, commercialize, and distribute the codebase in accordance with that license.

However, the Apache 2.0 license **does NOT grant any trademark or brand identity rights** to the Qyic project.

The following items are protected brand assets reserved exclusively for the original author and official maintainers:
* The official name **"Qyic"** and direct derivative names (e.g., *Qyic2*, *Qyic Pro*, *Qyic Mobile*).
* The official Qyic logos, app icons, splash screens, mascot/art assets, and primary visual identity.
* The official website branding, domain names, and official social media handles.

## 2. Forking, Naming, and Re-branding Rules

We actively encourage community forks to develop their **own distinct identity**. If you compile, build, modify, or redistribute this application publicly or commercially, you must follow these guidelines:

### A. Naming Guidelines
To prevent consumer confusion and protect against lazy clones acting like the original project, naming your fork is governed by the following rules:

* **✅ ALLOWED — Phonetic / Spirit Names:**
  Names that subtly reference or sound similar to the original project but establish a clear new identity are permitted.
  * *Example:* **Qyic → Quik** 

* **❌ PROHIBITED — Sequential / Prefix / Suffix Clones:**
  Names that add numbers, version tags, or light modifiers to the original name are strictly prohibited, as they imply an official continuation or tier.
  * *Prohibited Examples:* **Qyic2**, **Qyic-Plus**, **Qyic Next**, **Qyic Web**, **Qyic Community Edition**

### B. Logo and Visual Branding Guidelines
Forks may pay homage to the original design, but must remain clearly distinguishable:

* **50% Similarity Threshold:** Derivative logos or app icons are permitted only if **at least 50% of the design elements** (shape, composition, palette, geometry, or iconography) are uniquely original to your project.
* **Prohibited:** Exact copies, simple color inversions, or minor tweaks to the official Qyic logo that make it appear as an official variation.

### C. Distribution & Misrepresentation
1. **No Misrepresentation:** You may **not** state or imply that your fork is the official "Qyic" app, or that it is maintained, sponsored, or endorsed by the official Qyic maintainers.
2. **App Store & Domain Listings:** You may **not** publish an app on Google Play, the Apple App Store, or web hosting platforms using "Qyic" as the primary application name or developer handle.

## 3. Accurate Compatibility Statements (Allowed Use)

You are always permitted to make truthful, factual statements regarding your project's origin and compatibility with Qyic:

* **✅ Allowed Statements:**
  * *"Built on the open-source codebase of Qyic."*
  * *"Compatible with Qyic .qdeck files."*
  * *"A community fork derived from the Qyic repository."*

* **❌ Prohibited Statements:**
  * *"The official Qyic Android client."*
  * *"Qyic Pro Edition."*
  * *"Powered officially by Qyic."*
